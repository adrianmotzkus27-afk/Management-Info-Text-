## Experience

Throughout my experience in digital management, I have worked with different communities, projects, teams, and online platforms.

Over time, I have gained experience in Discord server management, staff organization, administration, website management, community development, and project coordination.

However, my journey has not always been easy. I have experienced many negative situations within the management industry, including poor leadership, broken trust, unprofessional behavior, internal conflicts, and a lack of communication.

These experiences have taught me important lessons about responsibility, professionalism, teamwork, leadership, and trust.

I have learned that good management is not about having a high rank or controlling people. It is about taking responsibility, supporting your team, solving problems, and creating an environment where everyone can work together.

I believe that every experience, whether positive or negative, helps me improve and become a better manager.

**My experience has taught me what kind of management I never want to represent.**
